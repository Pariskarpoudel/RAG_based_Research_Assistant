2.2 Physics-informed neural network

A neural network for a two-dimensional longitudinal section of the Pianello hillslope, 𝜓𝜃(𝑥,𝑦,𝑡), is constructed with four hidden layers, each containing 150 neurons and nonlinear activation functions. The loss function combines the following contributions (Raissi et al. 2019; Karniadakis et al. 2021; Cuomo et al. 2022):

• Physics loss: penalizing deviations from the diffusion equation residuals,

• Initial and boundary conditions: enforcing observed or prescribed conditions at the slope surface, boundaries, and initial state

The present work focuses on the physics-constrained component of the PINN loss, while the data mismatch contribution is left aside at this stage. Training is performed using the Adam optimizer, iteratively reducing the composite loss.
